# Curriculum Source Register

This register documents all official sources, curricula, textbooks, and learning outcome codes incorporated in Samajh.

## 1. Verified Official Documents Read

1. **NCERT, Foundational Learning Study (FLS) 2022 Benchmarking Report**
   - Class 3 reading speed bands: Hindi meets band (35 to 54 cwpm), English meets band (35 to 53 cwpm).
   - Question taxonomy: factual recall, word meaning in context, inferential why/how, and main idea.

2. **Ministry of Education, NIPUN Bharat Guidelines (2021)**
   - National Foundational Literacy targets (Lakshya) for Classes 1 to 3.
   - Outcome codes and grade-level expectations.

3. **NCERT, Learning Outcomes at the Foundational Stage (2025)**
   - Year-by-year foundational learning outcomes for Grades 1 and 2, including dictation and decoding.

4. **NCERT, Learning Outcomes at the Elementary Stage (2017)**
   - Curricular expectations and learning outcomes for Classes 3, 4, and 5 in Hindi language learning.

5. **NCF Foundational Stage (2022) & NCF School Education (2023)**
   - Curricular goals CG-9 to CG-11; Language goals R1 CG-1 to CG-5.

6. **Basic Shiksha Parishad Uttar Pradesh NIPUN Tables & Class 3 Guide (2025-26)**
   - State outcome codes H101 to H516; state targets H206, H208, H306, H308.

7. **RSCERT Udaipur Hindi Teacher Guides, Classes 1 to 5 (2025)**
   - Rajasthan textbooks, lesson titles, and curricular goals.

8. **NCERT Textbook Catalogue 2025**
   - Sarangi (Classes 1 and 2), Veena (Classes 3, 4, 5). Chapters and contents verified.

9. **CBSE Circulars Acad-73/2024, Acad-12/2025, Acad-48/2025**
   - Prescribed textbook transitions for CBSE affiliated schools.
