# Architecture & Product Decisions

This register records design decisions, resolutions to ambiguities, and architectural choices made in Samajh.

| ID | Topic | Context & Options | Decision Made | Rationale |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D001 | Speech / Handwriting Recognition | Report 2 mentions automatic speech/handwriting recognition. Report 1 explicitly rejects it. | No automated speech or handwriting recognition. The teacher plays audio and marks words; teacher evaluates notebook dictation. | Direct alignment with Report 1 (Rank 1 source of truth). Preserves offline reliability and eliminates AI hallucination in child scoring. |
| D002 | Cloud Services vs 100% Offline | Report 2 suggested cloud backup, OTP, SMS. | 100% Offline. No network calls, no INTERNET permission declared, local encrypted AES-256-GCM backup export. | Complies with strict zero-network requirement and child safety / DPDP privacy principles. |
| D003 | Passcode vs PIN | Report 2 suggested 4-digit PIN; Report 1 requires 6-digit passcode. | 6-digit passcode hashed with PBKDF2 with Keystore backing and exponential back-off after 3 failed attempts. | Follows Report 1 security specification. |
| D004 | Class 3 Speed Target Display | NIPUN specifies 60+ cwpm with comprehension; FLS 2022 defines 35-54 cwpm as 'meets' band. | Display both targets side-by-side with clear official source labels. | Truthful presentation adhering to the honesty rule: never invent numbers; show official source labels next to each benchmark. |
| D005 | Hindi Printing Pipeline | Direct PDF rendering with `pdf` package misprints Devanagari conjuncts (e.g. rendering किताब as कतिाब). | Rasterize Flutter widgets using Flutter's native HarfBuzz text layout engine before feeding into A4 PDF pages. | Ensures 100% accurate Hindi conjunct rendering on printed worksheets. |
| D006 | Star Charts & Rankings | Report 2 suggested star charts. Report 1 strictly forbids student rankings. | No class star charts, leaderboards, or student-facing score screens. Milestone stars appear only on the individual child's printed sheet. | Prevents stigmatization and upholds child-first pedagogy. |
| D007 | Marathi Support Phasing | Report 2 lists Marathi as a launch language. Report 1 ships English and Hindi, preparing Marathi as a drop-in pack. | English and Hindi UI in P1; language pack architecture built so Marathi is a drop-in pack with verified Balbharati registry in P2. | Avoids unverified translations or unverified curriculum references. |
| D008 | Real Data vs Demo Data Gate | Real child data collection under DPDP 2025 requires verifiable parental consent. | Visible switch: "Demo mode" (default, fictional 3B class) vs "Real class mode", gated behind a legal review checklist. | Protects children's privacy by design. |
| D009 | Layout Scalability & Text Scaling | At textScale 1.3x and 2.0x on 320dp devices, fixed spacers cause RenderFlex overflow. | Replaced raw unconstrained Spacers in Onboarding, Check Flow, and Settings with LayoutBuilder + SingleChildScrollView + ConstrainedBox. | Guarantees overflow-free rendering across 320, 360, 411 dp at scales 1.0, 1.3, 2.0 in both English and Hindi. |
| D010 | Scoring Guardrails & Evidence Honesty | Children reading for very short periods (<15s) or erroneous timer inputs producing impossible speeds (>250 wpm). | Suppress words-per-minute numerical calculation and display "Not enough evidence: teacher review needed". Exclude "not sure" and "not reached" from attempted words. | Ensures educational integrity and prevents stigmatizing or misleading fluency scores. |
| D011 | Demo vs Real Scope Isolation | Ensuring demo data exploration never pollutes actual student data, while allowing teachers to test the app freely. | Segregated database queries via activeScope column and separate classIds; Reset Demo Class zeroes out demo checks and restores 10 fictional learners to unchecked without touching real records. | Prevents data corruption and upholds strict DPDP boundaries. |
| D012 | Ephemeral Audio Vault & Retention | Children's voice recordings are sensitive personal data under DPDP. | Audio is stored in encrypted vault only until teacher finishes marking, deleted immediately upon saving (and hard-purged after 24h). | Eliminates storage of identifiable children's voice recordings on mobile devices. |
| D013 | Zero OCR & Elimination of Photo Capture | Student notebook photos and OCR introduce storage overhead, potential biometric/handwriting leakage, and AI misclassification. | Completely eliminated OCR, camera dependencies, and photo storage. Dictation checks replaced by child-facing visual choice items. | 100% child privacy, zero image storage, zero OCR inaccuracies. |
| D014 | Supabase Cloud Sync with Row-Level Security | Teachers need optional cross-device backup for Real classes without compromising offline autonomy or data privacy. | Reconciled cloud sync: 100% offline-first by default; optional cloud backup disabled until teacher explicitly connects. Uses PostgreSQL in Mumbai with teacher-level RLS policies. | Preserves offline autonomy and strict DPDP boundaries while enabling optional multi-device backup. |
| D015 | Child-Facing Word Check in Student Mode | Teacher dictation marking screen placed assessment burden on teacher and lacked standardized distractor diagnostics. | Replaced teacher dictation marking with child-facing visual choice items in Student Mode immediately after reading passage. Writing-position items render on notebook ruled lines via CustomPainter (not image assets). Passcode protects teacher exit. | Standardized formative assessment, positive student experience without error marks or grades, zero image assets needed for handwriting alignment. |
| D016 | Multi-Level Progression & Weighted Scoring | Simple binary scores fail to distinguish confident mastery from lucky guesses, slow decoding, or self-correction. | 4-level progression (L1-L4) with provisional promotion thresholds in thresholds.json. Quiet stop after 2 consecutive failed levels. Teacher view shows raw score, weighted score (1.0 first try, 0.75 wrong-to-right, x0.5 for 3+ changes, x0.85 for >2x expected time, floor 0.5), and top 3 mistake tags. | Child sees only encouragement; teacher gains precise diagnostic evidence to target practice needs. |
| D017 | Regional Dialects & Pronunciation Inclusivity | Children speaking regional dialects (Awadhi, Bhojpuri, Rajasthani, etc.) should not be penalized as having reading or speech defects. | Added regional_variants.json with home dialect setting for classes and children. Accepted regional variants count fully correct, never appear in error tags, and distractors are prevented from using valid regional variants. | Linguistic equity and asset-based assessment aligned with NEP 2020 mother tongue principles. |
| D018 | Typing instead of OCR for spelling | Spelling needs real evidence of what the child can write, without photos or OCR. | The phone says a word (offline text-to-speech; the teacher says it if no voice is installed) and the child types it on a big on-screen Hindi or English keypad. A checker names the mistake (half letter dropped, short/long matra, bindu, nukta, look-alike letter, missing/extra letter). Three typing levels: simple words, matra words, half letters and bindu. | Spelling evidence with no camera, no image storage and no OCR errors. Word lists are original and await teacher review. |
| D019 | Picture questions for letter formation | Writing details (position on the line, top line, spacing, even letters) cannot be judged from typing. | Picture MCQs drawn by the app: four drawings of one word, one written well and three with a common problem. Position: above, below, crossing the line. Formation: no शिरोरेखा, letters spread apart, uneven letters; English: one letter back to front (b/d, p/q). English sits on the line; Hindi hangs from it. | Child finds the correct writing; teacher sees which writing problem the child could not spot. |
| D020 | Thumbs up / down self-rating | Teachers want to know how the child feels about each task, not only the score. | After reading aloud, after each word check item, each typed word and each teacher question, the child taps 👍 or 👎. Only the teacher sees it, beside the real result (felt good but wrong, felt unsure but right). Saved per item and as a summary; synced as student_self_rating. Ratings are never marks. | Shows over- or under-confidence, which guides how the teacher talks to the child. |
| D021 | Strict level gating from Level 1 (replaces the grade-level start and the 2-fail stop in D016) | Starting at the class grade let a child skip levels they had not shown; stopping only after 2 failed levels let a child move up without passing. | Every child starts at Level 1 in the word check, typing check and teacher questions. A level opens only when the one below is passed; the check stops at the first level not passed. Question levels: fact 1, word meaning 2, why/how and cause-effect 3, main idea 4; a question level passes at half credit or more. | Children are never shown harder questions before the easier ones are secure. |
| D022 | Level-weighted, time-adjusted marks (replaces the x0.85 slow rule in D016) | Right/wrong alone, and a flat slow-answer penalty, do not reflect difficulty or speed. | Marks = answer credit (1 first try, 0.75 corrected, half for 3+ changes; questions: correct 1, partly 0.5) x level weight (L1 1, L2 2, L3 3, L4 4) x time factor (1 up to the expected time, falling in a straight line to 0.5 at 3x the expected time). Promotion uses right answers only, so a slow but correct child still moves up. All values are provisional in thresholds.json. | Harder levels count more; slower answers earn fewer marks without blocking progress. To be calibrated with teachers in the pilot. |
| D023 | Visual identity and motion | The app worked but looked plain and screens changed abruptly; the brief asked for a playful child side, a calm teacher side and smooth motion that stays fast on low-end phones. | Keep the existing green, Kiran gold and paper colours, Inter and Noto Sans Devanagari (no new fonts or packages). Teacher dashboard opens with a green chalkboard header (greeting, date, class count). Shared tokens for spacing, radii and shadows in `theme.dart`; one motion file (`motion.dart`) with page slide-and-fade, tab fade, staggered card entrance, count-up numbers, growing bars and rings, press feedback, a springy answer select, a thumb pop and one confetti burst when the child finishes. Check steps animate in only (the old step leaves at once) so there is never a moment with two steps on screen. Every animation plays once and is skipped when Android "Remove animations" is on. Devanagari reading text no longer has letter spacing, which broke the top line. | Uses only Flutter's built-in animation classes, so no new dependency and nothing that loops in the background. Motion answers a tap or marks a real moment; reading screens stay still for concentration. |
| D024 | Pebble look and teacher profile | The user chose the "Pebble" sample: cream page, white rounded cards, pills. They found the first Today page too colourful. | Cream background, white cards without outlines, pill buttons, navy for the main action, green only for progress, gold only for the open tab. Today shows one class at a time (pills switch class) with one pebble per child checked; support groups use shades of one green. Child questions use a 2 x 2 grid of big word tiles when there are four short words. New full-page teacher profile (name, what children call the teacher, role, school, UDISE, block, district, state, years of teaching, mobile, email); schema v8 adds the columns. | Calm teacher screens and big, friendly child screens. The profile is not synced: it stays on the phone. |
| D025 | One thumbs rating at the end (replaces the per-item ratings in D020) | Rating after every question tired children. | The child taps 👍 or 👎 once, after reading, the word check and typing, before the well-done screen. The teacher sees it set against how the check went (reading at 85% accuracy and Level 1 passed in word check and typing). Older checks with per-item ratings still show them. | Fewer taps; the signal (does the child know how they did) is kept. |
| D026 | Shorter checks | Too many questions tire young children. | Word check: 3 items per level, pass 2 of 3 (was 4, pass 3). Typing: 2 words per level, pass both (was 3, pass 2). The longest possible run falls from 25 to 18 items, and level gating usually stops earlier. Provisional, in thresholds.json. | Less fatigue while keeping a level-by-level picture. |
| D027 | Offline voice for the typing word | On a real phone the typing word was not spoken: the phone had no Hindi voice that works offline. | The speaker now picks a voice installed on the phone over an online one, and when none works tells the teacher exactly how to install it (Settings > Text-to-speech > Google > Install voice data > Hindi), with the hold-to-see fallback. | The app cannot ship a voice itself without a recorded or licensed voice; this makes the phone's own voice work offline once installed. |
| D028 | The app marks the reading; the teacher rechecks (revises D001) | The user asked that the teacher no longer marks every word: the app should hear the reading and mark mispronounced words, and the teacher only rechecks. D001 had ruled out speech recognition. | After Student Mode, the marking screen runs the bundled offline Vosk Hindi model (vosk-model-small-hi-0.22, 42 MB, Apache-2.0) over the reading recording on the phone, listening only for the passage's words plus "unknown". The heard words are lined up with the passage (fewest edits): heard as itself = correct, heard as another word or unknown = read as another word, missing in the middle = skipped, missing to the end = not reached. Wrong words and low-confidence right words get an orange ring; the teacher listens and changes any mark. Marks are never applied over the teacher's own. English passages, iPhones, missing recordings and failures fall back to marking by ear, with a note saying why. Runs through the official vosk-android library from MainActivity.kt (the vosk_flutter package no longer builds on current Flutter). | Audio never leaves the phone. Published word error rate of this model is 21 to 25% on adult speech; accuracy on children's reading is unmeasured, so the teacher's check stays final until it is tested on real recordings. Adds about 45 MB to the app. |
| D029 | Class-wise Hindi questions for Classes 1-3 from NCERT outcomes | Word check and typing were the same for every class, and passages were only loosely linked to NCERT. | Hindi Classes 1-3 get their own word check (4 levels x 5 items) and typing lists. Each item is tagged with the NCERT outcome it checks: Foundational Stage learning outcomes for Classes 1-2, NCF-SE 2023 curricular goals for Class 3. Themes follow the units of Sarangi 1, Sarangi 2 and Veena; all text is original. Passage questions for Classes 1-3 are tagged too. Every child still starts at Level 1 (D021). Wrong "verified" chapter lists in registry.json were corrected. See docs/CURRICULUM_ALIGNMENT_HINDI_G1_3.md. | Questions match what NCERT expects in each class, and the expectation is traceable to a page. Items await teacher review. |
| D030 | Fully automatic marking, English too (extends D028) | The teacher still had to mark every word of English readings, and only got marks after opening the marking screen. | Bundled the offline English Vosk model (vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15, 41 MB, Apache-2.0). Marking starts as soon as the child stops reading and runs while the child does the word and typing questions, so the marks are ready when the teacher looks. The teacher only listens to the words with an orange ring. | Teacher time goes into the doubtful words only. The APK grows by about 41 MB. Accuracy on children's reading is still unmeasured. |
| D031 | The result follows the evidence (replaces the 'teacher review needed' rule in D010) | A noisy room or a short reading replaced the whole result with "Not enough evidence: teacher review needed", even for a child who read every word right. | "Not enough evidence" now means only that no reading was heard or marked. A noisy room, a reading under 15 seconds or an impossible speed mark the result as low confidence and leave speed out; the group still comes from accuracy and understanding. When the teacher asked no story questions, understanding comes from the child's Level 4 meaning answers in the word check. Mistake tags show readable labels. | Every finished check gives a usable result automatically, with confidence shown honestly. |
