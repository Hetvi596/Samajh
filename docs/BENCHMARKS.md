# Official Benchmarks & Reading Targets Register

Every benchmark and target displayed in Samajh is derived directly from official government publications. Where no official number exists, the app explicitly states so.

| Class | Official Speed Target | Official Comprehension Target | Official Source | Status in Samajh |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Class 1** | *None* | Reads short sentences of 4 to 5 simple words with age-appropriate vocabulary | Ministry of Education, NIPUN Bharat Guidelines (2021) | Fully implemented. No artificial speed target; evaluation compares child's baseline with decoding accuracy. |
| **Class 2** | 45 to 60 correct words per minute (UP: at least 45 wpm) | Retells main events and characters of a short story | NIPUN Bharat Guidelines (2021); Basic Shiksha UP H206 | Target table followed (resolving NIPUN example-story conflict in favor of the official target table). |
| **Class 3** | At least 60 correct words per minute (FLS meets band: 35-54 cwpm) | Answers 3 out of 4 questions correctly (fact, word meaning, why/how, main idea) | NIPUN Bharat Guidelines (2021); NCERT FLS 2022; UP H306, H308 | Both NIPUN 60 cwpm and FLS 35-54 cwpm shown with clear source labels. |
| **Class 4** | *None* | Reads narrative and informative texts with comprehension and self-correction | NCERT Learning Outcomes at the Elementary Stage (2017) | "No official number; compared with the child's own earlier checks." |
| **Class 5** | *None* | Reads diverse texts with critical thinking, inference, and vocabulary grasp | NCERT Learning Outcomes at the Elementary Stage (2017) | "No official number; compared with the child's own earlier checks." |
